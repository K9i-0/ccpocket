A few things to know.

Where do my agents run?

On your own computer through the self-hosted Bridge Server. CC Pocket connects directly to it. Your Codex, Claude, or Antigravity provider still processes requests under your existing setup.

Do I need all three agents installed?

No. Use whichever agents you have—Codex, Claude, Antigravity (agy), or any combination. You can toggle each agent on or off in Settings at any time.

What works when I’m offline?

Outgoing messages wait in a queue and send automatically when your connection returns (or when a usage limit resets), and missed updates catch up. Your computer must stay online for agents to keep working.

Is it free?

Yes. CC Pocket is free and MIT-licensed, with optional in-app Supporter purchases. You’ll need your own Codex, Claude, or Antigravity access.
