A few things to know.

Where do my agents run?

On your computer, through the self-hosted Bridge Server. CC Pocket connects to it. Your Codex or Claude Code provider still processes requests under your existing setup.

What works when I’m offline?

Outgoing messages wait in a queue. When the connection returns, they send automatically and missed updates are restored. Your computer must stay online for agents to keep working.

Is it free?

Yes. CC Pocket is free, with optional in-app Supporter purchases. You’ll need your own Codex or Claude Code access.
